What We Bring

I8 Lounge is a place where people can do the hobbies that miss or wish to try. A place where hobbies can be explored and indulged to the fullest with everything we have avaliable.

Computer cabinets and competitive rooms

Computer cabinets are a dying source here on the islands so we offer a place that has it ready. Competitive rooms where 5v5s and 6v6s can be held in a tournement manner.

PC Parts and Pc Builds

A dedicated department for just the PC lovers and builders so they dont have to rely on off island shops or pay shipping.

Console Couches and lobby

Don't you miss those days where you and a few buddies would group around a TV and play console games together, we offer that feeling of familiarity and memory of the good ol days.

Board games, Tabletop RPGs, and Card games

We have a wide variety of board games to choose from and a place to play them. Tabletop RPGs are also welcome here with rooms to host your sessions. Card games that have pods of friends all feeling the joy of imagination.

Indoor Skate Park and Skate Parts

Have fun with an indoor skate park that doesn't need to worry about the weather, and if anything happens to your boards or skates there is a Skate Part corner where you can customize and enhance your gear.

Merch and Cafe

A merch store to show off your love for hobbies and a cafe to relax and hang out with friends.

Events and Workshops

Regular events and workshops to learn new skills, meet like-minded individuals, and celebrate various hobbies.

Membership and Loyalty Programs

Exclusive membership options and loyalty programs that offer discounts, special access to events, and other perks for our regular patrons.
